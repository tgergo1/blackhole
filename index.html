What is being computed

Every pixel follows a null geodesic of the Kerr metric backwards from the camera, integrated in horizon-penetrating Kerr–Schild coordinates with an adaptive Dormand–Prince 5(4) method. The disk is the Novikov–Thorne relativistic thin disk with the Page–Thorne flux; each hit is coloured as a blackbody at temperature gT, where g combines gravitational redshift, Doppler shift and frame dragging. Nothing is faked: beaming, the photon ring, the shadow shape and the higher-order images all come from the geodesics.
